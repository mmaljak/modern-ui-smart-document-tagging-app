INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Policy Title: Acceptable Use and Data Classification Policy
Policy ID: ISP-2024-003
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Owner: Chief Information Security Officer
Review Cycle: Annual

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes requirements for the acceptable use of Acme Corporation's information systems and the classification of company data to ensure confidentiality, integrity, and availability of corporate information assets.

2. SCOPE

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, and temporary workers who access Acme Corporation's information systems, networks, or data, regardless of location.

3. DATA CLASSIFICATION

All company data must be classified into one of the following categories:

  3.1 Confidential
      Includes: trade secrets, financial forecasts, M&A plans, personal employee data, customer PII.
      Handling: Encrypted at rest and in transit; access restricted to need-to-know basis; cannot be stored on personal devices.

  3.2 Internal
      Includes: internal procedures, non-public project plans, internal communications.
      Handling: Not for external distribution; access limited to employees and authorized contractors.

  3.3 Public
      Includes: marketing materials, published press releases, public website content.
      Handling: No access restrictions.

4. ACCEPTABLE USE

  4.1 Employees must use company systems solely for business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties or violate this policy.

  4.2 Employees must not: install unauthorized software; attempt to bypass security controls; share login credentials; access systems they are not authorized to use.

  4.3 All company devices must have full-disk encryption enabled and comply with MDM enrollment requirements.

5. INCIDENT REPORTING

Employees must report suspected security incidents, breaches, or policy violations to security@company.com within 24 hours of discovery. Failure to report a known incident is a policy violation.

6. COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT

Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, and may result in civil or criminal liability. All employees must acknowledge this policy annually.

7. EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the CISO and must be documented in the Policy Exception Register.
